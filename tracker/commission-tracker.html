Savings page. The advance is paid in the month of the sale. The client's first premium is due on the effective date (the sale date if you leave it blank), then one a month. Payouts after the advance is paid off are shown in the month the client pays; the carrier may pay a few days or weeks later. Cancelled policies are not removed automatically, so delete a sale if it cancels.

Residuals. From the renewal date on, each premium the client pays earns the sheet's renewal rate (the 2nd and 3rd rate columns, for the years in Pay Years), paid monthly. $ renewal rates are $ × members per month. Products with no renewal rate on the sheet pay no residual.

How commission is counted. % rates: AV × your Associate rate × advance months ÷ 12, where AV = monthly premium × 12. $ rates (PMPM / Flat PM): $ × members × advance months. Products with no advance months listed (ACA) count 1 month. One-time products (annuity deposits): premium × rate.

Rates come from the printed commission sheet. You can change the rate or months on any line before saving if a number is different on your statement.
